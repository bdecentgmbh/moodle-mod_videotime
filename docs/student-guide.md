# Video Time — Student guide

A Video Time activity shows a video. Your teacher may add tabs with more content next to it.

## Watching

Open the activity from the course page and start the video in the player. Depending on the
activity, the description is shown above the video, and your teacher's notes below it.

Some activities show the video directly on the course page instead, like a label. You watch it
there.

## Tabs

If the activity has tabs, click a tab's name to see its content, for example **Information**,
**Chapters** or **Transcript**. All tabs share the same player, so switching tabs does not reload
the video.

- On a wide screen, the video is next to the tab content. Drag the divider between them to give
  the content more or less room. The **Watch** tab shows the video at full width.
- On a narrow screen, the video is above the tabs.

Clicking a time in a tab, for example a chapter or a line of the transcript, moves the video to
that point.

## Completion

The activity's completion conditions are shown at the top of the activity page. Depending on your
teacher's settings, you complete the activity by viewing it, by marking it as done, or by watching
the video for some time, up to a percentage or to the end. Some activities show only *Complete
the activity*.

## Resuming, skipping and the next activity

Your site may also offer these, if your teacher has turned them on:

- The video continues where you stopped when you come back.
- You cannot skip ahead beyond what you have watched. If you try, a message tells you how much of
  the video you have watched so far.
- Above the video, **Next:** with the name of an activity leads you to that activity. A lock
  shows that it is not available yet. Some activities take you there automatically when you have
  completed the video.
