# Video Time Live Player — Teacher guide

The Live player turns a Video Time activity into a live broadcast. Instead of a recorded video, the
activity shows a camera or a screen that a teacher shares from the activity page.

## Setting up a live activity

1. Add a Video Time activity to your course.
2. Check **Live feed**, above **Activity name**. **Video URL** is disabled while it is checked.
3. Save.

**Live feed** is only offered when your administrator has enabled the Live player and set up the
Deft response block. If you uncheck it later, the activity needs a **Video URL** or a media file
again.

## Broadcasting

On the activity page, teachers see a **Shared video** block. Its buttons:

- the camera button shares your camera and microphone;
- the screen button shares a screen or a window, which your browser asks you to choose;
- the stop button ends the broadcast.

Each source you open gets a preview in the block, with buttons to switch the broadcast to that
source, to mute or unmute its sound, and to close it. You can open both the camera and the screen
and switch between them.

Only one feed is broadcast at a time. Starting to share while somebody else's feed is live replaces
it, which needs the capability **Moderate video**.

If the block shows "The Video Time live player requires the admin to install Deft response block
and enable video bridging in the site settings", video sharing is not enabled on your site. Ask your
administrator.

## What participants see

Until a feed is shared, the activity shows a poster image. While a teacher broadcasts, participants
on the activity page see and hear the feed. When the broadcast stops, the poster image returns.
