# Video Time Chat tab — Student guide

Some Video Time activities have a **Chat** tab (your teacher may have given it another name). There
you can write messages to everyone in the activity while the video keeps playing next to it.

## Writing a message

1. Open the **Chat** tab.
2. Type your message in the box below the messages.
3. Click **Send**.

Your message appears with your picture, your name and the time. Messages from others appear while
you have the page open.

## Deleting a message

Click the bin icon on one of your own messages to delete it.

Messages are visible to all participants of the activity.
