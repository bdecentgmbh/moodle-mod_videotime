# Video Time Chat tab — Teacher guide

The Chat tab adds a **Chat** tab to a Video Time activity, where participants exchange short
messages while they watch. It requires the Deft response block on your site, which delivers new
messages to everyone who has the activity open.

## Switching it on

In the activity settings, under **Tabs**:

1. Check **Enable tabs**.
2. Check **Show tab** next to **Video Time Chat tab**.
3. Optionally enter a **Custom tab name**. Without one, the tab is called **Chat**.
4. Save.

The fields only appear when your administrator has enabled the tab for the site.

## What participants see

The messages of the activity with the author's picture, name and time, grouped by day, and a text
box with a **Send** link below. All participants share one conversation per activity; groups are
not taken into account.

Messages are Moodle comments. Participants can delete their own messages; users who may delete
comments can delete any message.
