# Video Time Information tab — Teacher guide

The Information tab adds a tab with your own text to a Video Time activity, for example a summary,
instructions or links. The text can contain images and files.

## Switching it on

In the activity settings, under **Tabs**:

1. Check **Enable tabs**.
2. Check **Show tab** next to **Video Time Information tab**.
3. Write the content in **Information tab content**.
4. Optionally enter a **Custom tab name**. Without one, the tab is called **Information**.
5. Save.

The content and the name can only be edited while **Enable tabs** and the tab are checked. The
fields only appear when your administrator has enabled the tab for the site.

Unchecking **Show tab** and saving deletes the tab's content.

## What participants see

A tab with your content, next to the video.
