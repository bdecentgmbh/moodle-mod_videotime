# Video Time Information tab — Administrator guide

The Information tab gives Video Time activities a tab with text written by the teacher. It is
enabled when it is installed.

## Enabling the tab

*Site administration ▸ Plugins ▸ Activity modules ▸ Video Time ▸ Video Time tab plugins ▸ Manage
Video Time tab plugins*: use the eye icon in **Hide/Show**. The arrows in **Order** set the position
of the tab among the other tabs.

## Settings

*Site administration ▸ Plugins ▸ Activity modules ▸ Video Time ▸ Video Time tab plugins ▸ Video
Time Information tab*

**Default** (checked by default): whether **Show tab** is checked for the Information tab in new
activities.
