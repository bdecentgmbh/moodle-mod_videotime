# Video Time Transcript tab — Student guide

Some Video Time activities have a **Transcript** tab with the spoken text of the video.

## Reading along

Open the **Transcript** tab. Each line starts with the time at which it is spoken. While the video
plays, the current line is highlighted.

Click a time to move the video to that point.

If the video has transcripts in several languages, choose one in **Language** above the text.

## Finding a passage

If your site has Moodle's global search, you can search for words from the transcript there. A
result opens the activity on the **Transcript** tab in the language of the result, starts the
video at that line and highlights the words you searched for.
