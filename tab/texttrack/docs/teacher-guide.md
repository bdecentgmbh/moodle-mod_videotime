# Video Time Transcript tab — Teacher guide

The Transcript tab adds a **Transcript** tab to a Video Time activity. It shows the text of the
video's captions or subtitles with time stamps; participants click a time to jump to that point.
The tab requires Video Time Premium.

## Switching it on

In the activity settings, under **Tabs**:

1. Check **Enable tabs**.
2. Check **Show tab** next to **Video Time Transcript tab**.
3. Save.

The checkbox only appears when your administrator has enabled the tab for the site.

## Where the transcript comes from

- The text tracks of the Vimeo video, which Video Time Premium fetches from Vimeo.
- The text tracks of the type **Captions** or **Subtitles** uploaded in the activity settings under
  **Text tracks**.

The tab reloads the tracks when it is shown after the activity was saved, the video changed on
Vimeo or a text track file was uploaded.

## Several tracks

With more than one track, participants choose one in **Language**. Each track is listed with its
label or, without a label, with its language, followed by **CC** for captions and
**(auto-generated)** for tracks that Vimeo generated automatically.

Text tracks that are not **Visible** in the activity settings are only shown to users who may manage
files in the course, marked **(Hidden)**.

## Editing a track

Teachers who may manage files and activities see an **Edit** button below the tracks that were
uploaded in the activity. It opens a form for the track's cues.

## What participants see

The selected track, one line per caption with its start time. Clicking a time moves the video to
it, and the line being played is highlighted. If your site uses Moodle's global search, transcript
lines can be found there, and a search result opens the activity on this tab at that line.
