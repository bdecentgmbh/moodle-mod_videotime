# Video Time Chapter tab — Teacher guide

The Chapter tab adds a **Chapters** tab to a Video Time activity. It lists the chapters of the
video; participants click a chapter to jump to it. The tab requires Video Time Pro.

## Switching it on

In the activity settings, under **Tabs**:

1. Check **Enable tabs**.
2. Check **Show tab** next to **Video Time Chapter tab**.
3. Save.

The checkbox only appears when your administrator has enabled the tab for the site.

## Where the chapters come from

- For a Vimeo video: the chapters defined for the video on Vimeo. If it has none, the chapters
  text track of the activity is used.
- For other videos: the activity's visible text tracks of the type **Chapters**.

The tab shows the activity name as its heading and, for each chapter, its title and start time.

## Editing chapters

Teachers who may manage files and activities see an **Edit** button in the tab. It opens a form
with one row per chapter: a start time and a title. The chapters are saved as the activity's
chapters text track.

## What participants see

A list of chapters next to the video. Clicking a chapter moves the video to its start. While a
VideoJS video plays, the current chapter is highlighted.
