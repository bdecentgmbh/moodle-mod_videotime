# Video Time Watch tab — Teacher guide

The Watch tab is the main tab of a Video Time activity that uses tabs. It shows the video at full
width, with the activity's **Notes** below it.

## Switching it on

The Watch tab has no setting of its own. It appears in every activity where **Enable tabs** is
checked (under **Tabs** in the activity settings), as long as your administrator has the tab
enabled for the site. It is enabled and placed first when Video Time is installed.

## What participants see

When participants open the activity, the Watch tab is selected. On the other tabs the video stays
next to the tab's content, and the divider between them can be dragged to change their widths. It
is the same player throughout, so playback continues when participants switch tabs.

On narrow screens (up to 768 pixels wide) the video is shown above the tabs, and the Watch tab
shows only the notes.

The *Video Time — Teacher guide* describes tabs in general.
