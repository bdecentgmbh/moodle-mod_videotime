# Video Time Related activities tab — Teacher guide

The Related activities tab adds a tab to a Video Time activity that lists the activities and
resources of the same course section, with a button to open each. You can add a text above the
list.

## Switching it on

In the activity settings, under **Tabs**:

1. Check **Enable tabs**.
2. Check **Show tab** next to **Video Time Related activities tab**.
3. Optionally write a text in **Instructions for tab**.
4. Optionally enter a **Custom name**. Without one, the tab is called **Related activities**.
5. Save.

The fields only appear when your administrator has enabled the tab for the site.

Unchecking **Show tab** and saving deletes the instructions.

## What participants see

Your instructions, then one card for each activity and resource in the section of this Video Time
activity, with its icon, its name and an **Open** button. The current activity is included and
highlighted.

The list contains every activity of the section; it does not check whether a participant can see
or access each one.
