# Video Time Interactions tab — Student guide

Some Video Time activities have an **Interactions** tab. While you watch, the activity can show you
information, ask you to confirm that you are still watching, and ask you questions.

## Information

Some content appears in the **Interactions** tab for a part of the video and disappears afterwards.

## Continue prompts

A message with a **Continue** button and a countdown ("Time remaining … s") appears below the tabs.
Click **Continue** before the time runs out; otherwise the video pauses. Prompts with the text
"Click to continue watching video" can appear at any time while the video plays.

## Questions

When a question appears, the activity switches to the **Interactions** tab. Depending on your
teacher's settings, the video keeps playing, pauses until you have answered, or always pauses.
With the default settings, answer and click **Check** to see the feedback and whether your answer is
correct. When the question's part of the video is over, the question disappears and your answer is
kept.

A video in fullscreen leaves fullscreen when a prompt or a question appears.

## Starting again

If the activity has questions, the **Reset** button in the **Interactions** tab erases your answers
to all questions of the activity. Confirm the dialog, and you can answer them again.
